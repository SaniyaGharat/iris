# Key insights (Delivered orders only)

- **Top 5 categories:** Electronics leads with ₹4.31M (38.8% of revenue), and the top two categories (Electronics and Home & Kitchen) together earn 68.3%.
- **Monthly revenue:** Revenue averaged ₹0.80M a month from January to September, then stepped up 65% to ₹1.31M a month in Q4, peaking in October at ₹1.42M.
- **Top 3 cities:** Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru generate 55.2% of revenue, with Mumbai alone contributing 19.9%.
- **Revenue leakage:** Only 66.4% of order value (₹11.11M of ₹16.73M) was delivered; ₹5.62M was cancelled or returned.
